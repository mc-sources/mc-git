# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-06_

## 1. No Data Collection

Mc-Git is a **local desktop application**. It does not transmit any usage data, telemetry, or analytics to any server. The application functions entirely on your machine; the only network traffic it generates is the Git operations you explicitly initiate (clone, fetch, push, pull) toward the remotes you have configured yourself.

## 2. Feedback Channel

If you choose to send feedback through the in-app **Feedback** dialog, the application opens your default e-mail client with a pre-filled message addressed to **mcgit-feedback@martingatignol.fr**. The message contains the text you wrote and the application version. **No e-mail is sent without an explicit action from you** — you remain in control of the content and the recipient.

## 3. Processing of Feedback E-mails

E-mails received at `mcgit-feedback@martingatignol.fr` are processed by the maintainer for the sole purpose of **improving Mc-Git** (triaging bug reports, evaluating suggestions, answering questions). They are stored in a standard e-mail mailbox and are not forwarded to any third party. They are kept for as long as needed to follow up on the related issue, and may be deleted afterward at the maintainer's discretion.

## 4. GDPR Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) to **access, rectify, or delete** the personal data contained in any feedback e-mail you have sent, and to **object** to its processing. To exercise any of these rights, send a request to **mcgit-feedback@martingatignol.fr** referring to the original e-mail. Requests are processed within a reasonable delay.

## 5. No Other Collection

Outside of the voluntary feedback channel described above, **Mc-Git does not collect any data** — no crash reports, no usage telemetry, no opened-repository tracking, no fingerprinting. Local preferences (theme, language, recent repositories, credentials) are stored in standard application directories on your machine and never leave it.

## 6. Cookies

Not applicable — Mc-Git is a desktop application and does not use cookies.

## 7. Updates to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the application's data-handling behavior changes. Any update will be reflected in the date at the top of this document. Material changes will be announced in the project's `CHANGELOG.md`.

## 8. Contact

For any privacy-related question, contact the maintainer at **mcgit-feedback@martingatignol.fr**.
